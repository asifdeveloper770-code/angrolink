# AgriLink Connect

Create a complete, professional, ultra-responsive modern corporate web application for "AgriLink Kakuma LLC", an advanced agtech, logistics, and market intelligence social enterprise operating in Turkana County, Kenya. 

The application must feature a stunning "Modern Corporate & Clean Functional" design style, incorporating smooth GPU-accelerated hover effects, subtle entrance animations (Framer Motion style), floating data-pixel background motifs, and a high-contrast color palette: Primary Forest Green, Secondary Accent Green, and Sun Gold (#E5A912) for primary CTAs.

---

### CORE ARCHITECTURE & PAGES (Build Fully Functional Components for All):

1. Home Page (The Anchor & Routing Hub):
   - Hero Banner: Bold, all-caps geometric sans-serif typography ("AGRI" style headers), mission statement, and prominent Sun Gold CTA buttons ("Partner With Us" and "Request a Quote").
   - Quick-Routing Section: Instant user-segmentation cards allowing visitors to self-identify ("For Institutional Partners/Donors", "For Commercial Buyers/Off-Takers", "For Local Producers/Transporters").
   - The Three Business Pillars (featuring custom line icons for People, Truck, and Graph): Aggregation, Logistics, and Market Intelligence.
   - Live Impact Metrics Counter Grid: Real-time animated counters for "Total Metric Tons Aggregated", "Active Farmers in Network", and "Logistics Routes Secured".

2. About Us / Our Impact Page:
   - Deep institutional trust narrative detailing the founding story, environmental resilience mission, and climate-smart agricultural framework in Kakuma.
   - Professional team and advisor bios highlighting academic and policy credentials.
   - Downloadable resources section for annual impact reports and whitepapers.

3. Services / Operational Pillars Page:
   - Detailed interactive cards with hover effects breaking down:
     * Agricultural Aggregation (Smallholder Crop Consolidation, Quality Grading, Solar-Powered Cold Storage, Producer Training).
     * End-to-End Logistics (First/Last-Mile Transport, Cold-Chain Logistics, Cross-Border Freight, Fleet Management Integration).
     * Market Intelligence (Real-Time Commodity Pricing, Supply/Demand Forecasting Maps, Climate Fragility Reporting, B2B Digital Linkage).
   - Interactive Regional Map graphic showing trade corridors and coverage across Turkana County.

4. Partner With Us / Intake Portals (Utility Hub Page):
   - Multi-Sided Interactive Onboarding Forms with conditional routing states:
     * Farmer Registration Portal (Low-bandwidth optimized questionnaire for crop types, location, and harvest estimates).
     * Commercial Buyer / Off-Taker Inquiry Form (Bulk quote requests, volume requirements, delivery frequency).
     * Logistics Partner Application (Truck owner-operator onboarding form).

5. Knowledge Hub / Blog Page:
   - Searchable and filterable grid layout for publishing crop reports, market pricing updates, sustainability whitepapers, and local community impact stories.

6. Contact Us Page:
   - Secure corporate contact form, official business emails, operational phone numbers, and physical office location details (Kakuma / Nairobi).

---

### GLOBAL FEATURES & UI REQUIREMENTS:
- Navigation Header & Footer: Complete with logo placeholder, page links, language toggle button (English, Swahili, Arabic placeholders), and high-visibility "Partner With Us" action button.
- E-Commerce / B2B Catalog Simulation (Shop, Product View, Cart, and Checkout Modal/Page): Include a clean functional B2B sample product listing view for wholesale agricultural goods, enabling users to add commodities to a quote cart and proceed through a simulated corporate checkout flow.
- Performance & Responsiveness: Fully optimized for mobile phones, tablets, and desktops, ensuring clean layouts, generous whitespace, and zero layout shifts.
